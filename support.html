Getting started

Place the battlefield

Open the game and choose Deploy Battlefield. To move it, select Place Battlefield, pinch the gold edge and drag. Use two hands to move or rotate it. Choose Lock Battlefield when you are ready.

Aim and fire

In Free Aim, look above the board and pinch to start aiming. Move your hand to adjust the point, then release to fire. Recenter returns the remembered depth to the middle. If you prefer a visible target plane, choose Classic Grid and select Near, Center or Far.

Protect your cities

The nearest available battery fires automatically. Each battery has 18 charges per wave. Enemy hits reduce city integrity, and explosions too close to buildings can also damage them. Batteries reload between waves; cities do not automatically repair.

Common questions

Why didn't my pinch fire?

Keep the pinch while adjusting the aim point, then release. A canceled gesture, a paused game or missing tracking does not launch a shot. If the initial selection ray is unavailable, switch to Classic Grid.

How do I change the audio?

Music and effects have separate controls. Set either one to zero to mute it. The game pauses when you leave the immersive space.

Is the browser preview the same as the Vision Pro game?

No. The browser preview is an earlier, standalone version with mouse controls. Move the mouse to aim, release a click to fire, and use the wheel or keys 1–3 to change depth. It does not reproduce RealityKit graphics or headset input.
